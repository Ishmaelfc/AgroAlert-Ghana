🛰️ Satellite-Powered Drought Detection

AgroAlert pulls weekly NDVI data from Sentinel-2 and land surface temperature from MODIS via Google Earth Engine, combined with ERA5 weather reanalysis to build hyperlocal drought stress forecasts at the community level.

📞 Voice Alerts in Local Languages

When drought risk exceeds the threshold, AgroAlert automatically places voice calls to illiterate farmers in Twi, Ewe or Dagbani — and sends SMS alerts in English to literate farmers and community leaders.
